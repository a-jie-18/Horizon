### [iPhone 18 Pro Review: Refinement Over Reinvention](https://sspai.com/post/115308) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Oct 4, 07:58

**「Background」** After the iPhone 17 Pro&\#x27;s full redesign, Apple&\#x27;s iPhone 18 Pro shifts to filling gaps rather than reinventing, polishing existing experience and addressing the previous generation&\#x27;s regrets. The changes are hard to spot at first glance—only a smaller Dynamic Island and new colors signal it&\#x27;s a new phone—yet the author argues this generation re-sorts the Pro experience with a variable-aperture main camera, a 2nm A-series chip, and a tripled VC vapor chamber.

**「Solution」** The most tangible change is weight: the 18 Pro rises from 204g to 211g, and the Pro Max from 231g to 249g, buying the largest battery yet \(4056mAh and 5391mAh, the latter up nearly 12%\) plus the tripled vapor chamber and variable-aperture mechanics. Apple shrank the TrueDepth area by moving the infrared camera under the display, halving pixel density above it while doubling remaining pixel brightness; the author reports no visible difference and unchanged Face ID speed. The 48MP main camera gains a six-blade variable aperture with four stops \(ƒ/1.48, ƒ/1.8, ƒ/2.8, ƒ/4\), the widest admitting roughly 50% more light by area than ƒ/1.78. In the author&\#x27;s testing, sharpness peaks at ƒ/2.8, then ƒ/1.8, with ƒ/4 slightly diffracted and ƒ/1.48 softest; ƒ/1.48 suits low light and close subjects, ƒ/1.8 is the auto default, and ƒ/4 keeps group shots in focus or creates starbursts. New Pro-level controls add aperture, shutter speed, white balance, histogram, and manual focus, though the author finds them awkwardly placed and slow to adjust for fleeting moments. New Photographic Styles add texture \(skin softening, glow, film\) and a grain toggle, and video gains post-capture Cinematic mode up to 60fps, 4K Dolby Vision HDR timelapse, and improved audio separation. Battery life improves slightly—about 3% more after two hours of mixed use, 6–7% over a full day—and the larger vapor chamber prevents outdoor brightness drops. Charging supports USB PD 3.2 SPR AVS, negotiating ~18V and peaking near 43W versus the 17 Pro&\#x27;s ~37W, but power falls below 30W after 30% charge, so total charge time barely changes. The China model keeps dual physical SIM slots while adding eSIM, effectively allowing four stored numbers with two active.

**「Takeaway」** The author concludes that the iPhone 18 Pro is a solid, familiar answer rather than a reinvention: it offers a &\#x27;best of both worlds&\#x27; experience where users can ignore complex parameters or dive into Pro-level control, and remains the iPhone to choose for the most complete experience—while those wanting a new foldable form should wait for the iPhone Duo.
