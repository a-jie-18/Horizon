### [visionOS 27 Feature Roundup: Environments, Curved Windows, and AI](https://sspai.com/post/114901) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 23, 07:30

**「Background」** Apple released visionOS 27 on September 15 alongside its other major OS updates, bringing Apple Intelligence and Siri AI to Vision Pro along with a set of spatial-computing refinements. For existing Vision Pro owners, the question is which changes actually matter in daily use — especially since Apple Intelligence and Siri AI remain unavailable on devices sold in mainland China.

**「Solution」** The author walks through the release feature by feature. On the AI side, Siri can now appear anywhere in space and be summoned by gaze, and Visual Intelligence can answer questions about physical objects or window contents — though these are region-locked for now. The headline spatial addition is a new immersive environment called Thórsmörk, a real-shot Icelandic mountain scene with day and night variants, ambient winter audio, and flowing water. visionOS 27 also lets users upload their own panoramas into a &quot;My Environments&quot; folder, but the author notes these fall short of built-in environments because of limited capture resolution and field-of-view coverage, and because they lack motion and background sound — a nice-to-have rather than a replacement. Control Center is redesigned into three cards: a lock-screen-like card with time, battery, music, and collapsed notifications; a controls card for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Mac Virtual Display, and Focus; and an environment card for adjusting immersion. Curved windows arrive for Safari, Freeform, and Apple TV, though the author&\#x27;s hands-on test found the Apple TV app still would not trigger the effect in the release build. Safari adds a Side-By-Side tab view that tiles all open tabs in space, and Mac Virtual Display now supports previewing and editing 3D models via an enhanced Quick Look, including material overrides, wireframe and UV inspection, and annotations for team feedback. Recording gains 4K capture of up to three minutes of non-foveated view, viewable through a 4K mirror. Smaller updates include Freeform folders and spatial groups, faster Messages sync, a claimed up-to-3x faster Wi-Fi startup \(attributed to Apple, not independently verified\), extra-small widgets, and a Mac Virtual Display widget that connects even when the Mac is off. For developers, Safari can render 360° immersive web environments, and Reality Composer Pro 3 adds simulation, character animation, dynamic lighting, and Script Graph logic.

**「Takeaway」** The author&\#x27;s takeaway is that visionOS 27 is a steady, incremental upgrade whose most tangible wins are spatial conveniences — custom environments, curved windows, and 3D model review — rather than the AI features that remain unavailable to many users. It is most useful as a practical checklist for current Vision Pro owners deciding what to try first.
