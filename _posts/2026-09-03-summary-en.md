### [What We Really Pursue in Hi-Fi Audio](https://sspai.com/post/113159) ⭐️ 8.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 3, 03:32

**「Background」** The author initially dismissed Hi-Fi as pseudoscience, but later came to understand its technical foundations. With the rise of TWS earbuds and the removal of headphone jacks, dedicated audio gear seemed obsolete, yet &\#x27;dongle&\#x27; DACs emerged as a niche solution. This article demystifies the engineering behind Hi-Fi and addresses the subjective nature of sound quality.

**「Solution」** The author explains that Hi-Fi is genuine electrical engineering: digital audio must be converted to analog by a DAC, then amplified to drive headphones. Amplifiers can introduce distortion, noise, or frequency-dependent effects due to impedance mismatches. Sound quality is not a single metric; frequency response curves, measured with artificial ears, are compared to target curves like the Harman curve, but they don&\#x27;t capture everything. Terms like &\#x27;soundstage&\#x27; and &\#x27;dynamics&\#x27; are subjective summaries of measurable phenomena, but they depend on the recording and listener preference. The author advises trying gear in person or buying used to reduce costs, and suggests EQ as a cheap fix. Ultimately, the goal is to find gear that suits your taste, not to chase the most expensive or objectively &\#x27;best&\#x27; equipment.

**「Takeaway」** Hi-Fi is both science and art: while engineering metrics matter, personal preference and the recording itself are equally important. The author concludes that the pursuit is about finding equipment that brings joy, not about chasing specs or price tags.

### [MUJI MH1 Headphone Review: Budget Sound with Tradeoffs](https://sspai.com/post/113998) ⭐️ 7.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 3, 07:00

**「Background」** KingTsui, from TDS Studio, typically overlooks lifestyle brands like MUJI for digital products, but a reader&\#x27;s tip about MUJI&\#x27;s headphone piqued his curiosity. After a quarter of intermittent use, he reviews the MUJI MH1, a budget over-ear Bluetooth headphone, to see if it meets basic needs for travelers seeking quick replacements.

**「Solution」** The MH1 features a minimalist design with two color options, a large on-ear fit that may cause pressure on non-flat heads, and a plastic-metal headband. Controls are physical buttons with clear logic but unintuitive voice prompts. Passive isolation is decent, but active noise cancellation is mild, mainly filtering low frequencies, with minimal wind noise. Connectivity is stable with AAC codec, and battery life is rated at 35-58 hours with fast charging. Sound quality is bass-heavy and rough, with recessed mids and artificial brightness, though the soundstage is spacious. The author notes the lack of app support and firmware updates, and suggests trying it in stores due to fit issues.

**「Takeaway」** The MUJI MH1 is a passable entry-level headphone that meets basic needs but doesn&\#x27;t excel in any area, making it a product you might discover while shopping rather than one to seek out. Its value lies in its practicality and honest performance within its price range.

She decided to switch to a flip phone for a year, seeking to reclaim her time and presence. The challenge was significant, especially during social gatherings, but she was motivated by the statistic that people spend 15 years of their lives on their phones.

**「Solution」** Her solution was straightforward: replace the smartphone with a flip phone. Initially, it was hard and awkward, but over time she found benefits. Without GPS, she learned to use maps and directions, which improved her navigation skills and gave her a sense of accomplishment. This change also helped her seek her own validation and move past difficult memories, allowing her to focus on what mattered.

**「Takeaway」** The author concludes that this journey made her happier and more fulfilled, as she was able to live life on her own terms. The experience highlights the potential mental health and productivity benefits of disconnecting from constant digital connectivity.
