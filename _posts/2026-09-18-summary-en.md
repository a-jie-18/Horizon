### [macOS 27 Golden Gate: Stability, Design Fixes, and the Intel Cutoff](https://sspai.com/post/114643) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 17, 07:00

**「Background」** Apple released macOS 27 Golden Gate on September 15 alongside iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and watchOS 27. According to 少数派编辑部, this cycle is less about headline features than about a system-wide cleanup, following the visual language and interface problems introduced with macOS 26.

**「Solution」** The most consequential change is hardware: macOS 27 drops Intel support entirely, so only Apple Silicon Macs can upgrade, and Rosetta 2 enters its final full-support release before macOS 28 limits it to Intel games. The author reports noticeably snappier animations and app responsiveness than macOS 26, comparing the feel to Snow Leopard. On design, Apple reworked Liquid Glass on macOS: a new slider in Settings &gt; Appearance lets users choose between more transparent glass and a more opaque frosted look, window corner radii are less extreme, and the floating sidebar returns to hugging the window edge, with defaults commands offered to restore the floating appearance and menu icons. Sidebar icon tinting returns, menu bar icons are pruned to reduce ambiguity, first-party app icons gain more layered depth, background apps show a gray Dock indicator, and the traffic-light controls pick up a liquid-glass treatment. Cross-device work improves too: iPhone Mirroring can now adjust aspect ratio and switch between iPhone and iPad layouts, which the author ties to the coming foldable iPhone Duo, while Sidecar finally supports direct tap and swipe control of macOS apps, similar to Luna Display, provided the iPad runs iPadOS 27 on the same Wi-Fi. AI features are described only briefly because Apple Intelligence has not launched in mainland China: Siri AI moves into Spotlight as a resizable, movable chat window, draws on personal data from Mail, Notes, Photos, and Files, and gains a standalone app with iCloud-synced history; visual intelligence arrives on Mac, and Safari adds &quot;Notify Me&quot; page-change alerts plus topic-based tab grouping. Other details include menu bar collapsing, system and app audio in screen recordings, faster Mail search, 5K 120Hz ultrawide support with persistent display arrangement, pull-to-refresh in system apps, removal of AFP, and a planned macOS 28 removal of Mac OS Extended \(Journaled\).

**「Takeaway」** The author&\#x27;s thesis is that macOS 27 is one of the rare recent releases centered on stability and performance rather than novelty, with the Intel cutoff and Rosetta 2 countdown as the changes practitioners must plan around.

### [Custom BeatBox Faceplate Sticker via AI Art](https://sspai.com/post/114451) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 17, 03:11

**「Background」** After a collaborative CD player faceplate design contest, many participants had artwork but no clear path to actually manufacturing it. The official reference dimensions were too sparse to show how a design would sit relative to the shell and disc, so the author set out to build a more reliable base for visualizing and producing a custom faceplate.

**「Solution」** The author scanned a physical BeatBox unit with a flatbed scanner, using a burned disc as a size reference, to create a &quot;virtual assembly&quot; base image that better reflects the relationship between the faceplate and the disc. For the design itself, AI image generation served as an inspiration and starting point, demonstrated here by migrating the look of an old Iomega Zip drive. The author is candid that the raw AI output was unusable: the disc proportions and aspect ratio were wrong, and the irregular edges could not be applied directly. Cleanup in an image editor was therefore essential—stretching, cropping, and cloning to fill the panel, using the pen tool to trace the window&\#x27;s inner and outer edges to preserve a sense of depth and allow later resizing and repositioning, and filling in the infrared receiver hole before adjusting it. After flattening the white border, rounded corners, and receiver hole and tuning the window size, the image could be overlaid on the scanned base for a preview close to the real result. The author then ordered a PVC sticker for the transparent panel from a Taobao printing vendor, supplying a full-color image and a dimensional reference; some vendors will merge these into a production drawing. The finished sticker is applied to the transparent cover.

**「Takeaway」** The author&\#x27;s point is that AI-generated art can seed a custom hardware design, but only after deliberate manual cleanup and a measured base image make it manufacturable. The workflow is a practical, low-stakes example of turning a rough generated concept into a physical product through ordinary tools and a print-on-demand vendor.
