### [Rosacea Explained: Symptoms, Differential Diagnosis, and Care](https://sspai.com/post/114641) ⭐️ 7.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 19, 07:00

**「Background」** Facial redness, flushing, and stinging are easily mistaken for aging, sensitive skin, or ordinary acne, so many people never realize they may have rosacea. The author, who both lives with the condition and has rotated through dermatology, notes that roughly 5.46% of adults worldwide show similar symptoms, yet most remain undiagnosed.

**「Solution」** The author explains rosacea as a disorder of neurovascular hyperreactivity: facial vessels dilate under triggers like temperature swings, sun, emotion, or spicy food but fail to contract normally, so transient flushing gradually becomes persistent central-face redness, with heat, stinging, and barrier damage. A comparison table separates it from acne \(which has comedones and spreads beyond the face\), seborrheic dermatitis \(itchy, yellow-scaled patches on oily zones\), and barrier damage \(temporary redness that resolves once irritants stop\), while noting these can coexist. Management is framed as long-term, phenotype-based care: trigger diaries over two to four weeks, physical sun protection, lukewarm cleansing and simple ceramide-rich moisturizers, and prescription topicals such as azelaic acid, ivermectin, metronidazole, or short-term vasoconstrictors, plus oral tetracyclines or low-dose isotretinoin for severe cases. For persistent redness and visible vessels after inflammation settles, IPL and vascular lasers can close abnormal vessels, though they need multiple sessions, maintenance, and carry risks of bruising, pigmentation changes, and scarring, especially in darker skin. The author stresses that no single therapy is curative and that early, sustained control is what prevents progression to phymatous changes.

**「Takeaway」** Rosacea is a chronic, relapsing condition best managed like a long-term disease rather than cured: avoid triggers, control inflammation with targeted medication, and use light or laser treatment only during stable periods. The realistic goal is not elimination but keeping symptoms dormant and the skin comfortable.
