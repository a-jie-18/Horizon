### [GNOME&\#x27;s Design Evolution and Its Lasting Controversies](https://sspai.com/prime/story/the-development-and-controversies-of-gnome-de) ⭐️ 6.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 21, 09:02

**「Background」** Linux desktop adoption has long hovered below 5%, though it recently passed 10% in North America, helped by Windows 10&\#x27;s end of support and the Steam Deck. New users still face the command line and distribution fragmentation, and whichever distribution they pick, the default desktop is likely KDE or GNOME—the latter deliberately challenging familiar habits.

**「Solution」** The author, a longtime GNOME user, traces the project&\#x27;s design arc. GNOME 1 \(1999–2002\) resembled Windows 95, with desktop icons, a taskbar, and workspaces already present; its Sawfish window manager allowed deep Lisp customization. GNOME 2 \(2002–2011\) introduced a configurable two-panel layout that proved intuitive and productive, became the default on most major distributions, and helped GNOME overtake KDE amid Ubuntu&\#x27;s rise—later inspiring MATE and a still-shipping Classic Session. GNOME 3 \(2011–2021\) replaced GNOME 2&\#x27;s assembled panels with a unified GNOME Shell and its Activities Overview, addressing panel over-configuration, hard-to-find windows, inefficient hierarchical menus, and awkward workspaces. The Overview combines application launching, window previews, a dash, search, and vertically stacked dynamic workspaces that auto-create and remove themselves; the desktop lost its taskbar and desktop icons. The author notes the design&\#x27;s similarity to macOS Mission Control, but calls the integrated navigation a radical reworking of the WIMP paradigm. GNOME 3&\#x27;s release in April 2011 was rocky: the shift was not incremental, GNOME Shell crashed frequently early on, users defected, and MATE and Cinnamon emerged in response. GNOME denied ignoring feedback and promised no comparable upheaval again. GNOME 40+ \(2021–present\) moved workspaces from vertical to horizontal after usability research, and introduced Libadwaita, which decouples GNOME&\#x27;s Adwaita styling from GTK 4, improving GTK&\#x27;s generality and helping developers follow GNOME&\#x27;s human interface guidelines. The author also walks through current GNOME Shell usage—the top bar, Activities Overview entry via Super, hot corner, or three-finger swipe, search, dynamic workspaces, and Super-based window switching—while noting the piece ends mid-tour without a firm conclusion on whether GNOME is truly people-centered or out of touch.

**「Takeaway」** GNOME&\#x27;s history shows a desktop environment repeatedly trading familiarity for a coherent, opinionated vision, and paying for it in user backlash before settling into gradual refinement. The author leaves the central question—whether that vision is genuinely people-centered or detached from real use—open rather than resolved.

### [AI 让执行力贬值后，什么才是真正的优势](https://sspai.com/post/114519) ⭐️ 6.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 21, 03:38

**「背景」** 作者借用发展经济学的「中等收入陷阱」来类比 AI Agent 的发展：当模型能力普遍提升、同等智能的模型价格不断下降、办公类 Agent 让信息差消失，「会用 AI」本身将不再是稀缺能力。他由此提出一个思想实验——如果所有人都能用上最强的 Agent，你还有信心脱颖而出吗？

**「方案」** 作者把「会用 AI」拆成两类能力：一类是工具绑定型技巧，比如某代模型偏好的提示词写法、某个 Agent 该装什么 Skill、某套 Harness 怎么调教，这类优势依赖工具尚不成熟和用户间的信息差，会随模型、Harness 与大众经验的进步而持续贬值；另一类是认知与判断型能力，即知道真正的问题是什么、该给 Agent 什么上下文、什么结果才算好、何时该推翻它，Agent 越强，这种判断越被放大。他进一步区分「外部资本」（核心用户、社媒账号、可迭代的工作流等具复利性的积累）与「内在资本」（判断力与品味），并以 Apple 折叠屏过渡效果被开发者快速移植为例，说明「做出来」不等于「有人用」，外部资本的鸿沟难以被 AI 抹平。关于内在资本，他认为判断力遵循「预测→实际结果→误差→解释误差→更新模型」的循环，而直接接受 Agent 看似合理的答案会跳过异常值、无法更新判断；品味则更依赖大量接触与比较，可让 Agent 生成多个方向的候选项，再以用户视角比较分析。他也提醒注意力有限，应按重要性、长期投入回报、可迁移性做取舍，对 Agent 已做得更好的领域主动「退化」。作者坦承自己尚无太多实践经验，上述方法只是下一阶段打算尝试的方向。

**「启示」** 作者的核心论点是：AI 让执行力变得廉价，真正不随模型降价而贬值的，是外部资本的复利积累与内在的判断力和品味。与其追求把 AI 用得更好，不如设计一套机制，在与 Agent 的协作中持续创造自己的预测、异常值和比较对象，从而在长跑中保持不可替代性。

### [New Mac mini Review: M5 Pro and M6 in a Small Chassis](https://sspai.com/post/114829) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 21, 13:02

**「Background」** After nearly two years without an update, Apple&\#x27;s Mac mini returns with M5 Pro and, for the first time, the 2nm M6 chip. The author, upgrading from an M2 Pro model, asks whether this small desktop still earns its place as a home AI hub and light workstation.

**「Solution」** The redesign keeps the same compact body and port layout but swaps gigabit Ethernet for 2.5Gb, which the author notes only matters for NAS or server use. In loosely controlled comparisons against an M4 Pro MacBook Pro, the M5 Pro Mac mini cut LM Studio first-token latency on a Gemma 4 31B MLX 4-bit model from 0.384s to 0.157s \(about 2.45x\), while generation speed stayed flat at roughly 13.2 tokens/s — suggesting the neural accelerators mainly speed up prompt processing, not sustained output. A 10-minute 4K 60p ProRes 422 export finished in 62.3s versus 77.2s \(about 19% faster\), and a 1,525-image Lightroom batch preset took 3m17s versus 3m43s \(about 11.7% faster\). The author acknowledges the 82% original-export gap is storage- and cache-dependent and not a GPU measure. The AI-infrastructure framing leans on a vendor-commissioned Omdia survey and a Haidilao deployment anecdote, and the buying advice notes the base price rose from 4,499 to 6,999 yuan, recommending 512GB/24GB instead.

**「Takeaway」** The author concludes the new Mac mini remains a uniquely flexible Mac — home AI node, media server, or team compute unit — and that its M5 Pro gains are real but concentrated in prompt handling and batch workflows rather than raw generation speed.

### [派评：lire、Darkroom、Homebrew 等应用更新](https://sspai.com/post/114815) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 21, 09:57

**「Background」** This installment of 少数派&\#x27;s 派评 column rounds up recent updates to a set of apps spanning RSS reading, photo editing, life logging, input methods, package management, and fasting timers. The author frames these as release-note-level summaries aimed at readers who already use the specific apps, rather than deep technical analyses.

**「Solution」** The most concrete entries are lire, Darkroom, and Homebrew. lire&\#x27;s iOS and macOS clients add Apple Intelligence integration, including a Summarize button that uses Private Cloud Compute for full-text summaries with customizable prompts, plus a Translate feature for full-article translation \(target language set in Settings\). The developer also rebuilt the full-text extraction and caching engine and added a web login feature so paywalled content can be fetched, alongside a Modern list layout, an option to disable swipe navigation, and nine new app icons; it is a one-time $9.99 purchase with no subscriptions. Darkroom 7.4 extends its Liquid Glass UI to macOS and iPadOS, and updates the curve tool: the five vertical control points can now be dragged horizontally to adjust black and white points, and five HSL curves \(H/H, H/S, H/L, S/S, L/S\) bring video-style color grading to photos. White balance gains an Auto mode and a color picker, and RAW files now read the recorded color temperature as the initial slider value. Homebrew 7.0 adapts to macOS 27, demotes all Intel Macs to Tier 3 \(no new bottles, with full support ending September 1, 2027, and a recommendation to migrate to MacPorts\), drops macOS 10.15 and earlier, and optimizes install/update by starting downloads after fetching only necessary metadata. It also strengthens sandboxing and introduces a native Homebrew App \(installable via brew install homebrew-app on macOS 26+\) for viewing, uninstalling, checking, updating, and searching packages, plus an integrated Doctor. Other entries cover Time Atlas&\#x27;s automatic life-logging with maps, photos, and Apple Health data; 曦码输入法&\#x27;s Rime-based cross-platform input method with AI models and plugins; Fast Times, an open-source Android fasting timer; and brief notes on Ulysses, Things, and Tripsy.

**「Takeaway」** The roundup&\#x27;s value is mainly for existing users of these apps: it documents feature changes and platform-support shifts, but largely repackages release notes without first-hand engineering experience, measured evidence, or transferable technical insight.
