### [Monthly Game Roundup: Onimusha, Fire Emblem, Orbitals, and More](https://sspai.com/post/115056) ⭐️ 5.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 27, 09:50

**「Background」** This is a monthly preview column that independently selects and briefly reviews newly released games across PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, PC, and mobile, noting that coverage is not exhaustive and that each assessment reflects only the game&\#x27;s state at launch. The author stresses that not every title is a recommendation, and that games may improve or worsen through later updates.

**「Solution」** The roundup covers five titles. Onimusha: Way of the Sword revives Capcom&\#x27;s series after 2006&\#x27;s Dawn of Dreams, with producer Akihito Kadowaki and first-time director Ken Nihei citing the maturing RE Engine and a 2020 core team as reasons for the long gap; set in early Edo Kyoto, it is linear rather than open-world or soulslike, and modernizes the signature &quot;Issen&quot; counter with a stamina/balance gauge that enables a stronger &quot;Kuzushi Issen.&quot; Its protagonist, a young Miyamoto Musashi, uses the series&\#x27; core Oni Gauntlet to absorb souls and enter the Genma realm, and Capcom modeled his face on the late actor Toshiro Mifune after two years of negotiation. Fire Emblem: Fortune&\#x27;s Weave continues Three Houses&\#x27; world under directors Toshiyuki Kusakihara and Genki Yokota, moving from mutually exclusive routes to four protagonists whose paths converge, though RPG Site&\#x27;s Cullen Black found the 161-hour structure repetitive. Orbitals, Shapefarm&\#x27;s first original game, is a two-player-only action adventure with cel-shaded 3D animation mimicking hand-drawn &quot;on twos&quot; timing, and Kotaku&\#x27;s Kenneth Shepard felt it was beautiful but derivative. Control Resonant shifts to melee ARPG combat as Dylan Faden, with director Mikael Kasurinen citing God of War&\#x27;s hit feel, while critics questioned its story; VGC reported Firebreak&\#x27;s poor sales and a €14.9 million impairment. The Blood of Dawnwalker, from ex-CD Projekt RED staff at Rebel Wolves, casts players as a day-human, night-vampire protagonist with 30 in-game days to save family, forcing quest tradeoffs.

**「Takeaway」** The author&\#x27;s throughline is that these five releases pair strong pedigrees and striking presentation with real caveats—repetitive structure, derivative design, or uneven combat and story—so the roundup is best read as a launch-window snapshot for readers already tracking these specific games rather than a source of transferable technical insight.

### [派早报：OpenAI 称与苹果合作效果不佳](https://sspai.com/post/115079) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 27, 23:03

**「背景」** 这是一篇中文科技新闻早报，汇总了多条彼此独立的行业动态，而非围绕单一技术洞见展开。多数条目只是对公告、诉讼和传闻的简短事实性转述，其中 F-Droid 2.0 与 Excel 数组功能包含少量具体技术细节，其余则多为厂商声明与媒体报道的再包装。

**「方案」** 在 OpenAI 与 SpaceXAI 的反垄断案中，OpenAI 为反驳马斯克关于其与苹果达成排他协议、阻碍竞争的说法，援引诉讼文件称接入 Siri 的 ChatGPT 服务「持续表现不佳」，多步骤授权确认流程抬高了交互门槛，导致用户使用意愿偏低；文件显示该合作早在 2025 年 1 月便显露疲态，公司随后下调了该渠道的每周活跃登录用户增量预期，并称即便按原告数据模型计算，其在苹果生态中的市场封锁份额也微乎其微。文件还披露 OpenAI 曾要求两年独家合作但被苹果拒绝，最终协议为非排他性，苹果已转向与谷歌合作、基于 Gemini 构建模型。其余条目中，F-Droid 2.0 以 Kotlin 与 Jetpack Compose 全面重写，借助 DMA 推动的系统接口开放引入统一安装程序与预审批 API，实现后台自动更新与静默安装，重构搜索内核以改善中日韩文字检索，并将最低系统要求提升至 Android 7；Excel 则允许单元格原生保存列表与数组，新增 FLATTEN、HAS 等函数，但数据透视表、Power Query 等尚不能理解数组值，嵌套数组还需升级到兼容性版本 3。此外，三星冰箱固件更新失误导致部分设备屏幕与制冷功能瘫痪，微软在新款 Surface 宣传中淡化了 Copilot+ PC 品牌，以及若干被标注为「看看就行」的苹果硬件与 Vision Pro 传闻。

**「启示」** 这篇早报的价值在于快速浏览多条行业动态，但多数条目停留在转述厂商声明与未经独立核实的传闻层面，能提供的持久、可迁移的技术理解有限。
