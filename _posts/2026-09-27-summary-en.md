### [IKEA&\#x27;s Matter Rollout and Its Cautious China Strategy](https://sspai.com/post/114958) ⭐️ 6.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 26, 06:00

**「Background」** IKEA&\#x27;s first Matter smart-home products launched overseas over half a year ago, but China had seen no news until several new devices appeared in the national CCC certification database mid-year. As a global furniture retailer, IKEA has long taken a distinctive, restrained approach to smart home, evolving around the home scenario rather than piling on specs. The article draws on an interview with IKEA global product line lead David Granath and product developer Stjepan Begic to explain the shift from Zigbee to Matter and how IKEA plans to enter China&\#x27;s highly fragmented ecosystem.

**「Solution」** In the Zigbee era, IKEA kept things simple: most devices retained Touchlink direct pairing, so a buyer could use a bulb and remote without a gateway, while the TRÅDFRI and later DIRIGERA gateways handled remote control and bridged devices into Apple Home, Google Home, and Alexa. With Matter and Thread maturing, IKEA announced full Matter adoption and released 21 budget-friendly products—bulbs, switches, sensors, plugs, plus European thermostats and kitchen cabinet drivers—and plans to migrate its line over time. Setup improves markedly: instead of toggling power six times to reset a bulb, users scan a QR code. IKEA also hides complex automation behind tuned defaults. Early months brought dropouts, pairing failures, and latency, which Granath attributes to the architectural shift: IKEA went from end-to-end control of device, gateway, and software to being one node dependent on third-party ecosystems, Thread, and CSA standards. Joint debugging with ecosystem vendors and firmware iterations reportedly brought stability and responsiveness to a usable level. For China, Granath stresses caution and local relevance: Xiaomi and Huawei keep semi-closed ecosystems, OPPO and vivo lack home hubs and Thread border routers, and Samsung SmartThings has left the core market, leaving Apple Home as the main Matter backer—yet Apple TV and HomePod penetration is limited, so pure Thread products could arrive with no network to join. IKEA&\#x27;s first CCC-certified imports therefore prioritize kits with Touchlink remotes that work without any Thread infrastructure. Granath says global volume dilutes R&amp;D and certification costs, and a dedicated cost team keeps Matter pricing at or below Zigbee levels. IKEA is also reassessing local ecosystem partnerships after its earlier Xiaomi collaboration. On hardware, engineer Stjepan Begic cites the switch to built-in rechargeable batteries in new sensors, trading some size for longer life and compatibility with IKEA&\#x27;s Ladda batteries, and says IKEA contributes features and specs back to open source and the CSA, including work on Matter audio.

**「Takeaway」** IKEA&\#x27;s bet is that technology should hide behind the product, not become the user&\#x27;s first problem—so it is pairing low prices and simple setup with native Apple Home and Matter openness. Whether that logic transfers to China, where established ecosystems and incomplete Matter infrastructure shape habits, remains an open question.
