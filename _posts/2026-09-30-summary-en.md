### [ColorOS 17 Review: Malleable Daylight](https://sspai.com/post/114728) ⭐️ 6.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 29, 03:28

**「Background」** ColorOS 17 arrives as Android vendors chase Apple&\#x27;s glass-and-light design language while Apple itself quietly retreats from it: iOS 27 reportedly strips Liquid Glass of real-time highlights, edge refraction, and adaptive transparency to fix readability and power complaints. The author, an AI practitioner and long-time ColorOS user, reviews the system after about a month of hands-on use, focusing on visual design, motion, and on-device AI rather than standardized benchmarks.

**「Solution」** The centerpiece is the refreshed &quot;凝光视效&quot; \(condensed-light\) design, which finally unifies first-party controls — bottom bars, highlighted keys, tabs, core buttons — under a light-led paradigm of gradient shadows, nested blur, and highlights, with the strongest glow on bottom bars and real-time highlight projection preserved in dense layouts like the dialer and calculator. Fluid Cloud abandons its heavy black block for dark glass with Monet color-picked highlights, progress-bar glow, and ripple effects on track changes. An OPPO engineer explains the reasoning: light at the bottom with content pushed up guides the eye and balances the layout, while a &quot;mass&quot; concept governs blur and glow so 2D light mimics real object shadows and anchors springy feedback. The author finds the result constrained by readability-first engineering: tiles still rely on blur plus acrylic plus edge highlights, so small buttons and desktop folders can&\#x27;t carry advanced effects, light effects fade on light backgrounds, and dark mode turns lively light into heavy gray blocks — a deliberate, less aggressive tradeoff than iOS 26&\#x27;s Liquid Glass. Motion is praised for zero-latency press deformation tied to finger inertia, fast rebound, and a force-feedback framework, plus notification/control-center swipe animations where position and acceleration shape tile rebound curves; but calculator and privacy dialer pages lack the new motion, ordinary components have abrupt highlight transitions, and long-standing issues persist — no continuous small-window curve in landscape, mismatched corner radii, and an odd app-open parabola that sometimes feels absent and sometimes overpowering. Background retention improves \(the author&\#x27;s tracking app and QQ no longer get killed or drain half the battery\), and battery life is noticeably better than late C16 builds, though the author defers quantitative testing to others. On AI, Xiaobu&\#x27;s new power-button animation replaces the Siri-style marquee with a white light expanding into a blinking, swaying cartoon dot — the author&\#x27;s favorite change, arguing it makes AI feel human rather than cold and threatening. Xiaobu Space, Xiaobu and Me, and the memory symbiosis engine turn Xiaobu Memory into a system-level capability, feeding AI briefings and chat with user context; Pro/thinking modes are removed, and PCC post-quantum encryption with Intel Xeon 6 TDX protects cloud-side AI data. Multimodal gains include an offline model, improved scanning/translation, and an AI retoucher that rescues hazy or night photos without detail tearing. Xiaobu Next advances a multi-agent architecture — a president agent dispatching subagents, a hidden &quot;小影&quot; agent profiling usage, built-in tools, MCP support, and a terminal that can run Python and PC-style plugins — with the author crediting community feedback for pushing it toward on-device models and privacy safeguards. Accessibility and elder-care updates include smarter TalkBack label recognition, real-scene Q&amp;A with voiceprint memory, a large-type Xiaobu care mode, and AI anti-fraud with face-swap and call-scam detection.

**「Takeaway」** The author argues that glass-style design is fundamentally limited on phones because they lack a Z-axis, so readability and power costs force compromises — Apple&\#x27;s own iOS 27 pullback proves the point — while the more meaningful frontier is AI that earns trust through human-feeling interaction, on-device privacy, and inclusive design. ColorOS 17&\#x27;s light effects and Xiaobu&\#x27;s warmer persona are steps toward that, even if the glass itself remains a beautiful but constrained material.

### [Apple Watch Series 12 Hands-On: Health Sensing and Slow Charging](https://sspai.com/post/115061) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 29, 07:44

**「Background」** Apple&\#x27;s Series 12 and Ultra 4 introduce a rebuilt health sensing system, a new Readiness Index app, and a ceramic case option. The author, upgrading from a 45mm Series 9 to a 46mm Series 12, wore the watch for over a week to test whether the new hardware and software actually change daily use.

**「Solution」** The core change is a redesigned back sensor: electrode and optical heart rate sensors are rearranged into a tighter array with larger green LEDs, backed by aluminum and zirconia ceramic instead of sapphire. Apple claims this enables background heart rate sampling every 5 seconds — 60 times the previous rate — and up to 24 times more frequent HRV measurement, split into a new recovery HRV for stress and recovery alongside overall HRV. These feed the Vital Signs app and the new Readiness Index, a 0–10 score combining recent workouts, training load, vital signs, and sleep, updated through the day across four levels. The author&\#x27;s own checks found the green LEDs always active in 5–10 daily spot checks, and fewer sleep disruptions from sensor light than on older models. However, his Readiness Index stayed at 5–7 because the watch rated his sleep mediocre — an average score of 80, with interruptions scoring only 5/20 — even though he felt fine training and swimming, leading him to treat the score as a secondary reference. Battery life is unchanged at up to 24 hours \(10 hours outdoor, 25% more than Series 11\); on a workday from 7am at 100%, he returned at 7pm with about 48% after an hour of nap sleep tracking and half an hour of fitness use. His charging test found the China-market Series 12 reaching only 2.8W with the screen off and about 1W with it on, versus 4.5W and 1.25W for a Series 9 with its fast charger — roughly double the speed. Ultra 4 rises to 50 hours regular and 84 hours low power, with new extended training modes up to 25 and 45 hours.

**「Takeaway」** The author concludes the Series 12 is a comfortable upgrade for existing Series users and a better fit for outdoor athletes in Ultra form, but warns that the larger 46mm case nearly swallowed his slim wrist, and that the watch&\#x27;s sleep and readiness scores can diverge from how you actually feel.
