**「Takeaway」** The AQUOS R9 exemplifies Sharp&\#x27;s &\#x27;efficiency-first&\#x27; philosophy, offering a well-rounded mid-range flagship that prioritizes practical improvements over groundbreaking features. ### [Checking Anxious Fantasies Against Reality](https://www.reddit.com/r/selfimprovement/comments/1vthjf1/i_realized_how_important_it_is_to_check_my/) ⭐️ 4.0/10

reddit · r/selfimprovement · /u/ma9leb · Aug 20, 12:01

**「Background」** The author describes a common cognitive distortion: in social situations, anxiety can trigger automatic fantasies that feel real, such as believing others are laughing at or judging you. This can lead to withdrawal and reinforce negative self-perceptions.

**「Solution」** At a wedding, the author noticed this reaction when women around him were giggling. Instead of succumbing to the anxious fantasy, he stayed present, reestablished contact, and continued talking. He discovered that the women were actually warm and interested, contradicting his initial fears. This experience taught him the importance of tolerating the gap between anxious thoughts and reality, and gradually rebuilding his response by checking fantasies against actual behavior.

**「Takeaway」** The author concludes that it is crucial not to treat anxious fantasies as facts, but to stay in contact with reality long enough to verify them. This practice can help correct cognitive distortions and improve social interactions.
