### [2026 Range Hood and Gas Stove Buying Guide](https://sspai.com/post/112775) ⭐️ 7.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Aug 29, 01:20

**「Background」** In 2026, both range hoods and gas stoves in China are transitioning to new national standards, but at different times: gas stoves adopted the new standard in March, while range hoods will follow in November. This staggered transition creates a confusing market where old and new standard products coexist, making it easy for buyers to make poor choices. The author, drawing on work experience, aims to help readers navigate this period by explaining the key changes and providing practical selection criteria.

**「Solution」** The author explains that the new gas stove standard raises the top energy efficiency threshold, causing many older models to drop from Grade 1 to Grade 2, and changes the test method for thermal efficiency, so a 70% rating under the old standard is not equivalent to the new one. For range hoods, the new standard introduces stricter odor reduction \(60% in 3 minutes\) and total pressure efficiency requirements, but until November, buyers must rely on traditional parameters: air volume and static pressure. The author provides a tiered parameter table and advises high-rise residents with shared flues to choose at least 1000 Pa static pressure. He then reviews five hood types—top suction, side suction, top-side dual suction, ultra-thin flush, and fully hidden—highlighting tradeoffs in aesthetics and performance. For stoves, he emphasizes thermal efficiency as the top priority \(≥70% for Grade 1\), warns against fake high-power models, and recommends enamel drip trays for easier cleaning. He also discusses useful features like gesture control, stove-hood linkage, and steam cleaning, while dismissing gimmicks like voice control. Finally, he extends to kitchen design trends: open layouts, refined workflows, hidden piping, unified materials, and appliance suites, with tailored advice for small, medium, and large kitchens.

**「Takeaway」** The author concludes that buyers should not chase the latest standards but avoid inferior old-stock models, prioritizing air volume and static pressure for hoods, and thermal efficiency and power for stoves. Ultimately, the guide emphasizes that modern kitchen design should prioritize integration and social spaces over raw performance, as even basic models meet daily cooking needs.

**「Solution」** The author shares three methods that worked for them. **「Takeaway」** The author&\#x27;s core thesis is that charisma can be systematically built through deliberate, repeated practice, whether by taking jobs that force interaction, immersing in low-stakes environments, or making small daily improvements.
