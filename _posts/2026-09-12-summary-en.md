### [Li i6 Owner Review: Choosing the Unremarkable EV](https://sspai.com/post/113605) ⭐️ 5.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 11, 06:50

**「Background」** In China&\#x27;s crowded new-energy vehicle market, a first-time buyer with a roughly one-year-income budget of about 250,000 yuan faced a familiar problem: nearly every mainstream model in that sweet-spot price band offers strong comfort features, so the real question was which tradeoffs to accept. The author test-drove the Xiaomi YU7, Zeekr 7X, Tesla Model Y, Zeekr 007GT, and Fangchengbao 5 before narrowing the field to the YU7 Standard, Model Y RWD, and Li i6.

**「Solution」** The author&\#x27;s central insight is that the Li i6 won not by standing out but by being &quot;normal&quot; — a car that quietly buries its value rather than displaying it. The YU7 impressed on styling and clever details but lost on rear/cargo space, cost-driven comfort cuts, and a large gap between Standard and Max driving feel; the Model Y offered the best driving experience of the group but was let down by restrained comfort features, modest battery and charging specs, and a workplace rule barring Teslas from the underground garage, which blocked cheap slow charging. The i6, by contrast, exceeded expectations on driving quality and delivered near-class-leading comfort at its price, backed by Li&\#x27;s 5C charging network. The author chose the single-motor RWD version in black with 20-inch wheels, white interior, and the rear entertainment screen plus streaming mirror \(10,000 yuan in options\), landing at 254,800 yuan before insurance \(7,289.56 yuan\) and halved purchase tax \(11,274.33 yuan\), for a total of 273,363.89 yuan. Delivery took about three weeks. On the road, the author runs the suspension low with sport steering and air suspension for a wagon-like feel, switching to comfort when passengers are aboard; NVH is described as top-tier for the class, with double-glazed glass, active noise cancellation, and notably quiet seat massage, screen, and seat motors. Real-world range of the displayed 525 km proved achievable, and 5C charging went from 5% to 80% in about 10 minutes at over 500 kW peak and roughly 380 kW average; even a 4C station did 12% to 98% in 22 minutes. Assisted driving handles highways well but remains limited in cities, and the author found hands-on-wheel detection weak. Criticisms include the controversial styling, a front trunk without soft-close, and a rear armrest lacking damping that newer L8/L9 models already have.

**「Takeaway」** The author argues that the i6&\#x27;s strength is being a rare &quot;not particularly unconventional&quot; EV — physical door handles, framed doors and mirrors, an independent instrument panel, and sensible interior design — and that this normality, plus Li&\#x27;s long-term charging investment, is why it sells over 20,000 units a month and is worth recommending to buyers who want a car they can hold onto.

### [Apple&\#x27;s iPhone 18 and Duo Keynote Omissions](https://sspai.com/post/114449) ⭐️ 4.0/10

rss · 少数派 \(生活方式与效率\) · Sep 11, 08:30

**「Background」** Apple&\#x27;s &quot;Surprise and Shine&quot; keynote, the first led by new CEO John Ternus, centered on the company&\#x27;s debut foldable, the iPhone Duo, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro, Apple Watch, and AirPods 5. Because a 90-minute keynote left many specifics unstated, the author combed official spec pages, support documents, and developer materials to collect what Apple did not mention.

**「Solution」** The author argues the Duo is positioned less as an &quot;Ultra&quot; above the Pro than as a new line like the iPhone Air: it keeps the core iPhone and foldable experience while cutting imaging and peak performance. Its camera matches the standard iPhone 17 tier rather than the Pro, lacking variable aperture, the 48MP telephoto, LiDAR, ProRAW/ProRes RAW, ACES/Apple Log 2, and spatial photo/video, with a 12MP front camera and no Action button. At 5.2mm unfolded it is Apple&\#x27;s thinnest iPhone, yet at 254g its heaviest, 89g more than the Air. Its USB-C port sits on the left when unfolded, and like the Air it is eSIM-only. Charging differs too: the 18 Pro may reach 60W with an AVS-capable 60W+ adapter for 50% in 15 minutes, while the Duo needs a 60W+ adapter without AVS and reaches about 50% in 20 minutes. For apps not updated for the Duo, Apple described three compatibility modes, with full inner-display use requiring the iOS 27.1 SDK. On the 18 Pro, the author notes the A20 Pro is 2nm, US Pro and Pro Max use different modems, eSIM-only models get larger batteries, and China&\#x27;s version offers the widest SIM options. The S11 watch chip derives from the A20 Pro with doubled 4GB memory, and audio intelligence relies on a Secure Exclave and Private Cloud Compute, launching later in English beta. Prices rose across new and old iPhones while trade-in values fell.

**「Takeaway」** The author&\#x27;s roundup suggests Apple&\#x27;s first foldable trades Pro-level imaging and performance for thinness and a new form factor, while rising prices and falling trade-in values make the upgrade calculus notably worse. Much of the detail remains hedged or drawn from official pages rather than first-hand testing.
